About Ration Seva

A digital Public Distribution System built for transparency and trust.

Our Mission

Ration Seva digitises the Public Distribution System so that every beneficiary knows exactly what they are entitled to, every shopkeeper records distributions transparently, and administrators get real-time visibility into the system. It replaces paper registers with verifiable digital receipts.

👤

For Beneficiaries

View your ration card, monthly quota and complete distribution history at any time.

🏪

For Shopkeepers

Verify cards, distribute the monthly quota once per month and generate digital receipts.

🛡️

For Administrators

Track system statistics, manage cards, review fraud flags and export reports.
