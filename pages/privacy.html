Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide during registration: name, Aadhaar number, phone number, address, family size and ration category. Distribution records are created when a shopkeeper issues ration against a card.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to operate the Public Distribution System: verifying eligibility, calculating monthly quota, preventing duplicate distribution and producing reports for administrators.

3. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Sessions use signed JWT tokens. Aadhaar numbers are masked in application logs. All production traffic must use HTTPS.

4. Data Retention

Distribution records are retained for audit purposes. Account data can be corrected by the user from the profile page, or by an administrator on request.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@rationseva.example.
