Frequently Asked Questions

How is my monthly quota calculated?

BPL and APL families get 5 kg rice, 3 kg wheat, 1 kg sugar and 1 litre kerosene per member. AAY families receive a flat entitlement of 35 kg rice, 15 kg wheat, 2 kg sugar and 3 litres kerosene.

Why can I only collect ration once a month?

Each ration card has a single monthly entitlement. The system enforces one distribution per card per calendar month using a unique database constraint.

What happens if my card is deactivated?

A shopkeeper cannot record a distribution for an inactive card. Contact your administrator to reactivate the card.

What is the fraud score?

Every distribution is scored by a Python machine-learning service. Scores above 0.70 are flagged for administrator review, but the distribution still completes normally.
